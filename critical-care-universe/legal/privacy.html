Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Applies to the Critical Care Universe web app (private beta)

Critical Care Universe (CCU) is a medical education and clinical-simulation app. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles, why, who else touches it, and what choices you have. It describes what the app does today, not what it might do in future.

Never enter real patient information. CCU is a teaching tool. Do not type names, hospital numbers, photographs, scans, lab reports or any other identifiable patient data anywhere in the app, including team-room chat and the ABG photo simulator. Every patient in CCU is simulated.

1. Who we are

Critical Care Universe is created and operated by Dr. Apoorva Chandra (“CCU”, “we”, “us”). We decide how the information described below is used. To reach us, see Contact or the Support page.

2. Information we handle

We have tried to separate what is actually collected from what stays only on your device.

2.1 Account and beta-access data (stored by us)

CCU is in a private beta. To use it you create an account and an administrator approves it. This involves:

  • Email address and password. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. Your password is processed by Supabase Auth and is not stored by CCU in any table we manage. Please use a password you do not use elsewhere.
  • Email-confirmation status and the technical records that Supabase Auth keeps to run sign-in (for example, timestamps and session tokens). Supabase may also record connection details such as IP address in its own logs; we have not changed or verified that logging.
  • Beta-access record: a small table row with your account ID, email address, an access status (pending, approved or rejected) and the date you signed up.
  • Administrator actions: when an administrator approves or rejects an account, a log line recording the administrator’s email, the affected email address and the new status is written to the backend function log.
  • Sign-in session kept in your browser’s local storage so you stay signed in (see 2.5).

Only administrators can see the list of beta accounts. You can read your own access record; you cannot read anyone else’s.

2.2 Emails we send

Confirmation, password-reset and similar account emails are sent through Supabase using the email-delivery service Brevo. Brevo receives your email address and the message content in order to deliver it. Brevo may record delivery events, and may track opens and clicks on these messages; we do not control that setting for transactional email.

2.3 Team rooms, shared hall and voice line (sent to other participants)

CCU includes a multiplayer “team” mode (shift codes, rooms, the shared hall and a team voice line). If you use it:

  • What is sent to others in your room: your display name (set on your device, default “Doctor”), a random device identifier generated by the app, your chosen specialty, sex and avatar look, your position and actions in the simulated hall, and any short team-chat messages you type (up to 240 characters).
  • What is not sent: your account email address and your password. The “Login ID” field in Settings is stored only on your device.
  • How it travels: messages are relayed through public, free message services (ntfy.sh and public MQTT brokers) and hall movement and voice use direct browser-to-browser (WebRTC) connections, which are set up through the same relay. These services are not run by us, and room topics are derived from the shift code. Anyone who knows or guesses a shift code could join that room and read what is shared in it. Do not put anything private or sensitive into team chat or into your display name.
  • Moderation: team chat is not currently filtered or moderated by us.

2.4 Microphone, voice line and voice orders

  • Team voice line: if you switch it on, your browser asks permission to use the microphone and your audio is streamed directly to the other people in the room using WebRTC. CCU does not record this audio or upload it to a CCU server. Because WebRTC connects devices directly, other participants (and the STUN services used to set up the connection, listed below) can see your network address.
  • Voice orders: the voice-ordering feature uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition. Where that exists, the recognition is performed by your browser or device maker (for example, Google for Chrome or Apple for Safari) under their own privacy terms, not by CCU. CCU receives only the resulting text, which is used on your device to place a simulated order.
  • You can refuse or revoke microphone permission at any time in your browser or phone settings. The rest of the app works without it.

2.5 Data kept only on your device

The app saves the following in your browser’s storage (local storage, session storage or IndexedDB). It is not sent to us. It stays until you clear it, use the in-app reset where available, or clear the site data in your browser:

  • sign-in session for the beta login;
  • settings (volume, graphics quality, motion), intro-seen flags and your recent cases;
  • your display name, specialty, avatar choices, a random device identifier and optional profile name;
  • Memory Lab study progress, review history, your own flashcard decks and settings, and an offline cache for that tool;
  • a cached copy of “Live updates” results.

CCU does not use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and we found no such tools in the app code.

2.6 Optional AI team assistant (not active in this version)

The code contains an optional “ask the team” assistant that can use an AI model (Anthropic’s Claude) through a small proxy service. It is switched off in this version: no proxy address is configured, so the app uses its built-in scripted team answers and sends nothing to an AI service. If it is enabled in future, only the question you type (limited to 600 characters), the type of question, which specialists are present and the simulated patient’s data (vitals, story, results, what has been given) would be sent, and this policy will be updated first.

2.7 Content lookups and other requests your device makes

  • Live updates look up published literature from the Europe PMC service using search terms only. No personal information is included, but the service sees your IP address like any website.
  • Fonts on the main app page are loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.
  • Reference links to external sites (for example guideline societies and PubMed) only load when you tap them and are governed by those sites’ policies.
  • Standalone simulator pages in the same website (outside the sign-in area) load code libraries from public content-delivery networks (jsDelivr and the Tailwind CDN). The ABG photo simulator lets you choose an image from your device and reads it with an in-browser text-recognition library; the code does not upload the image to us. That library may download its language data from a public CDN. Do not use images that contain patient information.

2.8 Hosting

The app is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log requests (for example IP address and browser type) as part of operating the service. We do not receive or analyse those logs as part of the app.

3. Why we use information

  • to create and secure your account, confirm your email and decide who can use the beta (account data);
  • to send account emails such as confirmation and password reset;
  • to let administrators approve, reject and manage beta users;
  • to run team rooms, the shared hall and voice features you choose to use;
  • to diagnose problems and answer support requests you send us;
  • to keep the service safe and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Sharing

We share information only as described above: with the service providers in the table, with other participants in a team room you join, and where the law requires it. Administrators can see account emails and access status. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

6. Security

The app and its sign-in connection are served over HTTPS. Account sign-in and the beta-access database are protected by Supabase authentication and row-level access rules, so a signed-in user can read only their own access record, and administrator actions are checked on the server. No online service can be made completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

Please note what we do not claim: CCU is not a medical-records system and is not designed, certified or assessed for HIPAA or similar health-privacy regimes. Team rooms use public relay services and are not private or end-to-end encrypted by us. Do not use CCU for real patient data.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Account and beta-access records are kept while your beta account exists. We have not yet set a fixed retention period.
  • Data on your device is under your control: clear the site data in your browser, or uninstall the app, to remove it.
  • Team-room messages are held by the third-party relay services under their own rules, which we do not control.
  • Deleting your account: an in-app “Delete account” button is not available yet. Until it is, email us from your registered address (see Contact) and ask us to delete your account and beta-access record. We will act on verified requests; copies in provider logs or backups may remain for a limited period under those providers’ practices.

8. Children’s privacy

CCU is for healthcare professionals and students of medicine and allied health. It is not directed at children, and you must be at least 18 to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Medical information and patient data

Educational use only. CCU is an educational and simulation platform. Its cases, patients, vital signs, results, scores, algorithms and any AI-generated or scripted team answers are for learning. They do not replace professional medical judgement, assessment of a real patient, institutional protocols or current clinical guidelines. Clinical decisions must be made by qualified healthcare professionals. Nothing in CCU is a diagnosis, treatment recommendation or emergency advice. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Because we do not want identifiable patient data, the app is not built to receive it. If you enter any, you do so against this policy and at your own risk, and you remain responsible to your patients, institution and the law. If you believe patient information was entered by mistake, contact us promptly so we can help remove what we control.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the personal information we hold about you, to object to some uses, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. You can ask for any of these through Contact; we may need to confirm your identity first. You can also:

  • decline or revoke microphone permission in your browser or phone settings;
  • avoid team rooms and the voice line entirely and still use the rest of the app;
  • sign out from Settings, and clear site data to erase everything stored on your device.

11. International services

The services listed above run on servers in various countries, and we have not restricted where they process data. This means your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including in countries with different data-protection laws. By using CCU you understand this. We have not verified the exact hosting region of each provider.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects or which services it uses (for example, switching on the AI assistant or adding account deletion), we will update this page and its “Last updated” date before the change takes effect.
