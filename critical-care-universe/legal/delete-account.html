Delete your account

Last updated: 27 November 2026

You can permanently delete your Critical Care Universe (CCU) account yourself, from inside the app. If you cannot use the app, you can ask us to do it by email.

This page cannot delete an account by itself. You do not sign in here. Deletion is done either in the app (Option 1) or by our team after you email us (Option 2).

On this page
  1. Option 1: delete in the app
  2. Option 2: ask us by email
  3. What deletion does
  4. What we cannot delete

Option 1: delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Critical Care Universe and sign in.
  2. Open Settings on the home screen and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the notice, tick the box, enter your current password, and type DELETE.
  4. Tap Delete my account. Wait for the “Your account has been deleted” screen.

Waiting for approval, or access not granted? You can still delete the account. On the “Your request is pending” or “Access not granted” screen, tap Delete my account under the Sign out button.

Forgot your password? Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen first, then follow the steps above.

Option 2: ask us by email (fallback)

The email route is a fallback for people who cannot use the in-app option, for example if you never confirmed your email, cannot sign in, or no longer have the app. It is not a replacement for the in-app option, which is quicker and needs no waiting.

Email a deletion request

Send it to criticalcareuniverse@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”.

  • Write from the email address registered to the account. We cannot act on a request that comes from a different address.
  • We must be sure the account is yours before deleting it, because deletion cannot be undone. We may reply with a question or ask you to confirm from the registered address. If we cannot verify ownership, we will not delete the account.
  • Do not send your password, and do not include patient information.

What deletion does

  • It is permanent. We cannot restore a deleted account.
  • You lose access with that account. To use CCU again you would have to register and be approved again.
  • Your account record is deleted from our servers: your email address, your password (held by our sign-in provider) and your beta-access status and date.
  • When you delete in the app, the app also erases the data saved on that device: sign-in session, profile, settings, recent cases and Memory Lab progress.

What we cannot delete

  • Data on your other devices. Each device keeps its own copy of settings and Memory Lab data. The app clears only the device you delete from. Clear the site data in your browser settings (or uninstall the app) on any other device. Memory Lab data was only ever stored on your device, so it is never held by us, and once cleared it cannot be recovered.
  • Team-room messages already sent. Team chat and room set-up travel through outside relay services (ntfy.sh and public MQTT brokers). Once sent, we cannot take messages back from those services or from other participants’ devices.
  • Logs and backups held by outside services. Our sign-in and hosting provider (Supabase), our email-delivery provider (Brevo), the website host (GitHub) and the relay services may keep technical logs or backups for a period set by their own policies. We cannot erase those on your behalf, and we have not verified how long each keeps them.
  • Emails already sent to you remain in your own mailbox.

The full list of services is in the Privacy Policy.

Before and after

  • Before deleting, note down anything you want to keep. CCU cannot export your Memory Lab progress.
  • Administrator accounts are protected and cannot be deleted from the app. Administrators should email us.
  • If deletion fails in the app you will see a message and your account will not be treated as deleted. Try again, or use Option 2.
  • Questions? See Support or write to criticalcareuniverse@gmail.com.
