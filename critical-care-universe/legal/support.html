Support

Help with sign-in, email verification, beta approval and technical problems

Critical Care Universe is in a private beta run by one person, so replies may take a little time. Most sign-in problems can be fixed with the steps below.

Medical emergencies: Critical Care Universe is a teaching simulator and cannot help with a real patient or emergency. Contact your local emergency services.

Privacy: never include patient names, records, images or any identifiable patient information in a support message.

Contact us

Email our support team and include the details listed under “Reporting a problem” below:

Email support

Support address: criticalcareuniverse@gmail.com

Please write from the email address you used to register so we can find your account. We aim to reply within a few working days. For how we handle your information, see the Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting

I did not receive the confirmation email
  1. Wait two or three minutes, then check Spam / Junk and, in Gmail, the Promotions and Updates tabs.
  2. The email comes from Critical Care Universe and the sending address may show a web address ending in brevosend.com. Search your mailbox for “Critical Care Universe” or “confirm”.
  3. On the “Check your email” screen tap Resend email (available about a minute after the last one).
  4. Use only the newest email. Each new email makes the earlier ones stop working. If you pressed Resend several times, open the latest message only.
  5. Check that you typed your email address correctly. If not, go back to sign in and register again with the right address.
The verification link says it is invalid or expired
  1. Links work once and expire after a while. Some email apps open links automatically to scan them, which can use the link up.
  2. Open the app address, tap Back to sign in, enter your email and password. If your email is not yet confirmed you will be offered a new email.
  3. Open the newest email on the same device and browser where you plan to use the app.
  4. If it still fails, contact support with the time you requested the email.
I cannot sign in or forgot my password
  1. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password and follow the email link to set a new one. Passwords need at least 8 characters.
  2. Make sure you are using the same email address you registered with and check for typing mistakes.
  3. Confirm your email first. You cannot sign in until the confirmation link has been used.
  4. If you see “Could not check your access”, you are probably offline. Connect to the internet and tap Try again.
  5. Try closing and reopening the app, or a different browser, if the sign-in screen seems stuck.
My account says “Your request is pending”

After you confirm your email, a team member must approve your beta request by hand. This is normal and can take some time. We do not send a separate email when you are approved.

  1. Leave the page open: it checks automatically and enters when you are approved. You can also tap Check status now.
  2. Or come back later, sign in and the app will open once approved.
  3. If you have waited several days, email support from your registered address.

If you see “Access not granted”, beta access has not been approved for that account. You can write to support if you think this is a mistake.

The app is slow, blank or a feature is not working
  1. Refresh the page, or close and reopen the app.
  2. Use an up-to-date browser (Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox) and a stable connection. The 3D scenes work best on a recent phone or computer.
  3. In Settings, set Graphics quality to low and turn on Reduce motion if the app feels slow.
  4. Team room or voice not connecting: check that everyone uses the same shift code and has an internet connection, and allow microphone access when asked. Some hospital or work networks block this kind of connection; try mobile data.
  5. Voice orders not working: speech recognition depends on your browser and device; it may not be available in every browser.
I want to delete my account or my data

Open the app, go to Settings → Delete account and follow the steps (current password, then type DELETE). If you are on the “Your request is pending” or “Access not granted” screen, tap Delete my account there. Deletion is permanent. If you cannot use the app, email support from your registered address and we will verify and process the request. Steps, limits and what is removed are on the Delete account page and in the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a problem

The more detail you send, the faster we can fix it. Please include:

  • what you were doing and what you expected to happen;
  • what actually happened, including any message on screen (a screenshot helps, but make sure it shows no patient information);
  • your device and system (for example “iPhone 15, iOS 26, Safari”) and the time it happened;
  • the email address of your account (never your password).

To report content you believe is wrong or unsafe, such as inaccurate clinical information, a bug in a simulation, or inappropriate team-chat behaviour, use the same email and describe where you saw it.

Medical disclaimer

Critical Care Universe is an educational and simulation-based platform. Scenarios, patients, treatment pathways, calculations and any scripted or AI-generated team answers are for learning only. They do not replace professional medical judgement, assessment of a real patient, institutional protocols or current clinical guidelines. Always verify against authoritative sources. Clinical decisions must be made by qualified healthcare professionals.
