# Guideline audit of Memory Lab cards, 23 November 2026

**Scope:** all 392 cards in 18 decks were read against current guidance. 72 were corrected or re-referenced and 18 were added (410 cards now).
**Status:** every card is still **Draft · pending review**. This audit was done by an AI assistant from published summaries and society web pages.
It is a prepared worklist for a clinician, not a clinical review, and nothing has been marked as reviewed.

## Needs a reviewer: newer guidance exists but the full text was not read
* **KDIGO AKI/AKD 2026**: only the public-review draft was found (comments closed May 2026). AKI cards still cite KDIGO 2012, with a note and one new card marked "draft". Check whether the final version has been published.
* **Baveno VIII** (J Hepatol 2026, pre-proof): variceal bleeding and TIPS cards cite Baveno VII; compare.
* **ATLS 11**: haemorrhage classes and the needle-decompression site were not found in the summaries read; cards cite ATLS 10 with a note.
* **Brain Trauma Foundation**: 4th edition (2016) is still the latest; a 5th edition is in preparation.
* **AHA 2025 adult ALS (Part 9)**: bradycardia and tachycardia algorithm cards still cite AHA 2020. Adrenaline timing differs between AHA 2025 (after initial defibrillation attempts fail) and ERC (after the third shock); the card states both.
* **WHO arboviral guideline (2025)**: dengue card cites the older WHO guidance.
* **GOLD**: the COPD card uses the 2024 report; GOLD is updated yearly.
* **Stroke 2026**: pre-lysis and post-lysis blood-pressure limits and the thrombectomy time/core windows are in the full text, which was not read; the card keeps the AHA 2019 BP values and says so.
* **DKA resolution**: the BHB <0.6 mmol/L with pH ≥7.3 or bicarbonate ≥18 criterion should be confirmed against Table 1 of the consensus report.
* **SSC 2026 hydrocortisone threshold** and vasopressin threshold: the web summary states only "suggest corticosteroids" and "add when doses escalate"; the 2021 numbers are retained and labelled as 2021.

## Not changed
Cards on physiology, equations, scoring systems, ventilator mechanics, airway technique, poisoning antidotes, electrolytes and drug doses were checked and left as they were, because no newer society guidance affecting them was identified. This does not mean they have been clinically reviewed.
