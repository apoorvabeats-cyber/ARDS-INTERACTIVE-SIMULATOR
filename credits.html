Adaptations

Selected anatomical surfaces were grouped, transformed and simplified. The stomach was scaled and aligned with the HRA assembly. Warm skin and tissue colours and conventional red/blue vessel colours are applied for display. These are simplified anatomical surfaces, without photographic textures. The assembly is an educational reference, not a patient-specific scan or clinically validated model.
