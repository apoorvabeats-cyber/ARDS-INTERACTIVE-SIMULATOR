Clinical Decision Tree
⚠️ Persistent severe hypoxemiaP/F 78. Review lung-protective ventilation and consider prone positioning.
ARDS severitySevere

Oxygenation

Target SpO₂ 88–95% and reassess P/F.

Lung protection

Target Vt 4–8 mL/kg PBW; Pplat <30.

Prone positioning

Consider prolonged prone positioning when P/F ≤150.

Rescue / escalation

If refractory, reassess reversible causes, rescue therapies and VV-ECMO referral.

How to go aheadContinue lung-protective ventilation and reassess.
  • Prone ventilation for severe ARDS with P/F ≤150 when appropriate.
  • Neuromuscular blockade may be considered in selected patients with severe dyssynchrony.
  • Inhaled pulmonary vasodilator can be considered as a temporary rescue/bridge for refractory hypoxemia, not definitive therapy.
  • VV-ECMO referral should be considered for refractory severe hypoxemia despite optimized conventional management.
  • Recruitment maneuvers are not routine; avoid prolonged/high-pressure maneuvers that cause harm.
