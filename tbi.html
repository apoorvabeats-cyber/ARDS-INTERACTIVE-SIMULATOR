TIER 1 — FUNDAMENTAL NEUROPROTECTION

Intervention Target / Action
Head position Head of bed approximately 30°, head and neck neutral.
Oxygenation Avoid hypoxemia; practical target SpO₂ ≥94%, PaO₂ generally ≥80 mmHg.
Ventilation PaCO₂ approximately 35–40 mmHg. Avoid routine prophylactic hyperventilation.
Blood pressure Avoid hypotension and maintain adequate cerebral perfusion.
Temperature Maintain normothermia, approximately 36–37.5°C.
Glucose Avoid hypoglycemia and severe hyperglycemia.
Analgesia / sedation Propofol or midazolam according to hemodynamics and clinical context, with adequate analgesia.
CSF drainage EVD drainage if present and appropriate.

TIER 2 — SECONDARY ICP CONTROL

Therapy Example
3% Hypertonic saline Common adult bolus approach: approximately 150–250 mL IV, guided by local protocol and sodium/osmolality monitoring.
Mannitol 20% 0.25–1 g/kg IV bolus with monitoring of renal function, volume status and osmolality.
CSF drainage EVD drainage according to neurosurgical protocol.
Neuromuscular blockade Selected patients with ventilator dyssynchrony, shivering or other contributors to ICP elevation; ensure adequate sedation.
Repeat CT If neurological deterioration or concern for expanding/surgical lesion.

TIER 3 — REFRACTORY ICP

Therapy Options
Deep sedation Propofol or midazolam according to hemodynamics and ICU protocol.
Neuromuscular blockade Cisatracurium, rocuronium or vecuronium when clinically indicated, with adequate sedation/analgesia.
Barbiturate therapy Pentobarbital or thiopental in selected refractory cases with appropriate hemodynamic and EEG monitoring.
Decompressive surgery Consider decompressive craniectomy in selected refractory cases with neurosurgical input.
REFRACTORY ICP
Urgent neurocritical-care and neurosurgical escalation.
