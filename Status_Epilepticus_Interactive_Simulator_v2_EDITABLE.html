Step 6 — Refractory Status Epilepticus

Seizures that continue after a benzodiazepine and an adequate second-line drug are refractory. Intubate, start continuous EEG, and begin an anesthetic infusion. Do not give a third benzodiazepine bolus instead.

Anesthetic infusion, after the airway is secured

Midazolam0.2 mg/kg bolus, then 0.05–2 mg/kg/h
Propofol1–2 mg/kg bolus, then 30–200 mcg/kg/min. Watch for propofol infusion syndrome.
If those failPentobarbital coma is the next anesthetic, not another loading drug from the same list.

Simulation Complete

Core pathway:

Recognize status epilepticus → stabilize ABCs → check glucose / reversible causes → benzodiazepine → second-line antiseizure medication → reassess → refractory status pathway if seizures persist → continuous EEG / ICU escalation → identify and treat etiology.

Key Learning Points

  • Treatment should begin rapidly once status epilepticus is recognized.
  • Benzodiazepines are first-line emergent therapy.
  • If seizures continue, rapidly administer a longer-duration antiseizure medication.
  • Persistent seizures after appropriate initial therapy require escalation.
  • Continuous EEG is important when ongoing electrographic seizures are suspected.
  • Always search for and treat the underlying cause.
