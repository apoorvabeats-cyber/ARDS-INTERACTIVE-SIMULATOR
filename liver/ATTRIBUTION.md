# Liver anatomy model

The file `liver-anatomy.glb` is the digestive model `public/anatomy/digestive_male.glb` from Anatria-3D (https://github.com/Nurkan1/Anatria-3D).

That model is derived from Z-Anatomy, which is derived from BodyParts3D.

- Z-Anatomy, Gauthier Kervyn and contributors. https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy. CC BY-SA 4.0.
- BodyParts3D, Database Center for Life Science. https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/. CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.

The learning lab shows the liver, the eight Couinaud segments, the gallbladder and the bile duct. The portal vein and the hepatic artery are not separate meshes in this file.

Teaching anatomy for the Critical Care Hub. Not a surgical plan and not a medical device. If you redistribute a modified model, keep this notice and the CC BY-SA licence.
