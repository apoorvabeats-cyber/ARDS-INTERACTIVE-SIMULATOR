IDSA Preferred First-Line Directed Therapy
Ceftazidime-avibactam (CZA) 2.5g IV q8h

Avibactam effectively inhibits OXA-48 carbapenemases. Cefiderocol is an alternative preferred agent.

Critical Guideline Pitfall / Ineffective Agents

Do NOT use Meropenem-vaborbactam or Imipenem-relebactam for OXA-48. Neither Vaborbactam nor Relebactam inhibit Class D OXA-48 enzymes, leading to treatment failure!

Alternative IDSA Agents
Cefiderocol 2g IV q8h (over 3 hours)
Antimicrobial Stewardship & De-escalation Path

Discontinue empiric Vancomycin and empiric double Gram-negative coverage immediately upon confirmation of directed therapy. Obtain follow-up blood cultures to document clearance.

Clinical PK/PD Pearl

Beta-lactam antibiotics exhibit time-dependent bactericidal killing. Extended infusions (3-4 hours) or continuous infusions optimize %T > MIC without increasing total daily dose, significantly improving clinical cure rates in critically ill patients with severe infections or elevated MIC organisms.
