Interpretation

All isolates, patients and results here are simulated for teaching. MICs are generated from an illustrative model and are not real laboratory data. A simulated organism's mechanism is a modelling input; in a real patient the mechanism must be confirmed by testing and the antibiogram is measured, not predicted. Content is educational and not for clinical decision-making.
