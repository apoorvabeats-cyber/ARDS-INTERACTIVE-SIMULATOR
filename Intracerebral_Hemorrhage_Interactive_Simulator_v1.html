Intracerebral Hemorrhage
Interactive Simulator

Acute spontaneous ICH assessment: ICH Score, hematoma volume, BP pathway, anticoagulant reversal, hydrocephalus/ICP and surgical escalation.

1. Clinical & CT entry

Educational decision-support tool. This simulator is based primarily on the 2022 AHA/ASA spontaneous ICH guideline. It does not replace neurocritical-care/neurosurgical assessment or local protocol.

2. Automatic ICH Score

ICH Score components: GCS, age ≥80, ICH volume ≥30 mL, IVH, and infratentorial origin. The score is a severity communication tool and should not be used alone to determine treatment limitation or prognosis.

3. Acute blood-pressure pathway

For mild-to-moderate spontaneous ICH with SBP 150–220 mmHg, the 2022 AHA/ASA guideline supports smooth, sustained BP control with a target of about 140 mmHg and maintenance in the 130–150 mmHg range; lowering below 130 mmHg is potentially harmful. Markedly elevated SBP >220 mmHg requires individualized intensive treatment and close monitoring.

4. Antithrombotic reversal

Exact dosing and eligibility for reversal agents require drug-specific timing, laboratory data, renal function and local formulary/protocol review.

5. Surgery, hydrocephalus & ICP

Brainstem compression / severe posterior-fossa effect
Ventricular obstruction requiring CSF diversion consideration
Cerebellar ICH ≥15 mL, neurological deterioration, brainstem compression or obstructive hydrocephalus are important triggers for urgent surgical evaluation. EVD is considered when hydrocephalus contributes to reduced consciousness. Supratentorial minimally invasive evacuation may reduce mortality in selected patients; functional benefit remains uncertain.

6. Final ICH decision

Guideline anchor: Greenberg et al., 2022 AHA/ASA Guideline for Management of Patients With Spontaneous ICH. Verify all treatment decisions against the current guideline, local protocol and specialist assessment.
