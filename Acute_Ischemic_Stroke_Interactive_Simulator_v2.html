5. Immediate monitoring

Neurologic monitoringFrequent neurologic reassessment and urgent evaluation for deterioration, severe headache, vomiting or acute hypertension.
After IV thrombolysisMaintain BP ≤180/105 mmHg for 24 hours and obtain follow-up CT/MRI at 24 hours before antiplatelet/anticoagulant therapy.
If EVT is performedContinue close neurologic and hemodynamic monitoring; avoid overly intensive SBP lowering after successful reperfusion.
Airway / physiologyAddress hypoxia, hypoglycemia, fever, aspiration risk and other secondary brain insults.
