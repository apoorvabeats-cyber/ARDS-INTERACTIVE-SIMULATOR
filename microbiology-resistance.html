Plasmid-borne genes such as blaNDM, blaKPC, mcr-1 and qnr move between cells, and between species, by these routes. Conjugation is the most important route in clinical resistance spread; transformation and transduction are less frequent in most hospital Gram-negatives.

Illustrative teaching model, not a clinical susceptibility prediction. A mechanism being present in a bacterium does not by itself predict the antibiogram: this depends on expression, the isolate's other mechanisms and the drug. Educational content.
