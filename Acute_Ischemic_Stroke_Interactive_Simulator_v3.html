5. Early post-reperfusion / ICU checklist

Neurologic & BP monitoringAfter IVT: neurological assessments and BP every 15 min during and after IVT for 2 h, every 30 min for 6 h, then hourly until 24 h. Maintain BP ≤180/105 mmHg.
24-hour imagingObtain follow-up CT or MRI at 24 h after IVT before starting antiplatelet or anticoagulant therapy.
Suspected symptomatic intracranial hemorrhageSevere headache, acute hypertension, nausea/vomiting or neurologic deterioration after IVT should trigger urgent assessment and emergency brain imaging.
IVT + EVTIf eligible for both, IVT should not be skipped solely to facilitate EVT, and EVT should not be delayed to wait for clinical improvement after IVT.
Post-EVT BPAvoid routine intensive SBP lowering to <140 mmHg after successful EVT; the 2026 guideline notes potential harm from intensive lowering.
