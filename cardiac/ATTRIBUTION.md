# Cardiac model

The file `heart.glb` is the cardiac subset extracted by the Cardiac Atlas Lab project from Z-Anatomy.

- Z-Anatomy, Gauthier Kervyn and contributors. https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy. CC BY-SA 4.0.
- BodyParts3D, Database Center for Life Science. https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/. CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan.
- Extraction, crop and export described in https://github.com/shimayuz/cardiac-atlas-lab (CC BY-SA 4.0 for the derived mesh).

The conduction drawing, blood-flow particles and cut plane in `cardiac-lab.html` are teaching overlays. They are not part of the atlas mesh.
