PHASE 4

Raised ICP / Intracranial Hypertension Risk Assessment & Tiered Management

Select applicable clinical and CT red flags. The simulator integrates them with measured ICP, if available. Anisocoria is a clinical warning sign for possible mass effect/herniation—not by itself proof of a measured ICP elevation.

Clinical Criteria / Red Flags

Important: anisocoria, fixed/dilated pupils and deterioration are clinical warning signs that increase concern for intracranial mass effect/herniation. They do not equal a numerical ICP value; correlate with CT and, when available, measured ICP.

CT Criteria

Current BTF threshold: treat ICP >22 mmHg. Clinical and brain CT findings may be combined with ICP values for management decisions.
