The file brain.glb and the labels in manifest.json are not original to this
lab. They are the Brain Project atlas, built from:

- Z-Anatomy, which is built on BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS)
- Deep nuclei and tracts registered from open MNI-space atlases, kept under CC BY-SA 4.0:
  CIT168 (CC BY 4.0), Najdenovska et al. 2018 thalamic atlas (CC BY-SA 4.0),
  Neudorfer et al. 2020 hypothalamic atlas (CC BY 4.0), HCP1065 tract templates

Source repository: https://github.com/itayinbarr/brainproject
Those registered nuclei and tracts are approximate, about 7 mm, for teaching only.
They are not a surgical plan and not a medical device.

If you redistribute a modified model, keep this notice and the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

The lab page itself (neuro-lab.html) is original application code for the Critical Care Hub.
It does not relicense the model.
